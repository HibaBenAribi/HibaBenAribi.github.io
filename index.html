About

Since graduating three years ago, my career has evolved from academic research into in-field work, ultimately blending the two.

My Master’s degree in epigenomics focused on how environmental factors influence human biology and health.

As a research assistant, I investigated the impact of diet and nutrition on human health while developing solutions for agricultural sustainability and waste valorization.

This foundation inspired a two-year shift into hands-on agricultural projects across diverse international ecosystems, which naturally sparked a deep commitment to wildlife conservation and biodiversity protection.

Today, I operate as an independent scientist, collaborating within various scientific networks on projects spanning biological research, computational biology, agriculture, and wildlife protection.

Projects

Agricultural & Biodiversity Field Projects

Arid Land Restoration

2025-2026

Dedicated one full year to restoring an arid farm abandoned for 50 years due to drought and severe soil degradation.

Rebuilt soil health, planted trees, and began converting the land into a permaculture design.

Restored a traditional Tunisian lime-and-stone eco-house with off-grid rainwater collection systems to establish a low-impact living model.

Wildlife Protection & Biodiversity Conservation

2026

Restored local wildlife habitats by cleaning natural areas, creating water resources, and ensuring safe coexistence, which brought back native foxes, birds, owls, and reptiles.

Completed field training with WWF North Africa in the savannah ecosystem of Bou Hedma National Park, learning professional monitoring and tracking techniques for endangered national species.

Highland Tropical Farming

2025

Spent two months on an organic farm in Malaysia practicing tropical fruit farming (Papaya, Coconut, Jackfruit, Banana, etc).

Focused on integrating livestock into farming, producing custom bio-fertilizers from manure, and managing plant nurseries through seed propagation and vegetative cuttings.

Lowland Tropical Farming & Community Engagement

2025

Spent two months in Indonesia practicing lowland tropical cultivation, focusing on coffee, rice, and tropical fruits.

Combined morning farm labor with afternoon community education including practical English communication skills and environmental awareness. In local schools, Christian churches, Islamic boarding schools, mosques, kindergartens, and informal gatherings.

Mediterranean Agricultural Ecosystems

2024

Spent three months across three distinct agricultural projects in Turkey : monoculture olive farm, a permaculture farm, and a mountain food forest.

This experience provided a direct comparison between the high-yield operational focus of monoculture, the soil-building practices of permaculture, and the climate resilience of multi-strata food forest systems.
